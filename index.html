A bit about me

I've spent 10+ years solving problems in tech.
Now I do it for people.

For as long as I can remember, I've been the person my circle turns to when something's weighing on them — a career crossroads, a relationship that doesn't make sense anymore, a decision they can't seem to make. I didn't plan it that way. It just became what I do.

Breaking things down, finding what's actually wrong, and cutting through the noise — that's been my approach in tech for over a decade. The same skill applies to people's lives just as much as it does to code.

Why not just use AI?

AI is designed to make you feel good. It will validate your thinking, soften its feedback, and tell you what you want to hear. I won't. You'll get an honest, objective perspective — even if it's uncomfortable — because that's the only kind that actually helps.

Why I'm doing this

This is not a business. I'm not here to scale, run ads, or build a brand. I deliberately stay off social media for this reason. I want to work with a small number of people, give them my full attention, and genuinely make a difference.
